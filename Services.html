Business Accounting Support

The Key Pillars of Our Services

  • Structuring & Guidance for In-House Accounting: For companies that choose to maintain an independent accounting department, we undertake its full design, organization, and ongoing scientific supervision.
  • Digital Transformation & Computerization: We contribute to boosting corporate productivity by proposing and configuring suitable ERP and software systems for the rational recording and processing of commercial and financial data.
  • Single-Entry & Double-Entry Bookkeeping: We provide comprehensive support to sole proprietors and entities (S.A., LLC, PC, etc.) required to maintain Category B or C books, ensuring total compliance with turnover limits and legal forms.
  • Financial Statement Preparation: We manage the issuance of all required accounting statements under Greek Accounting Standards (L. 4308/2014), taking into account your company's size, turnover, and workforce.
  • Payroll Management & HR Support: Payroll processing is a complex procedure requiring deep knowledge of labor, insurance, and tax law. Using modern, flexible software, we offer a clear picture of all payroll figures and employee obligations.

Tax Planning & Advisory

▼
In an environment where tax regulations are constantly revised...
  • Company Incorporation & Setup: We plan and execute all necessary steps for starting any corporate entity.
  • Advisory & Tax Support: We analyze the specifics of every professional or business and design a customized tax plan.
  • Preparation & Submission of Tax Returns: We handle the accurate filing of forms E1, E2, and E3 for individuals, sole proprietorships, and corporations.
  • Real Estate Tax Management: Full guidance on taxing income from real estate calculated on legal tax brackets.
  • Withholding Tax Statements: We manage withholding and tax remittance at source based on income type.
  • Tax Audit Support: In collaboration with legal experts, we stand by you during audits (AADE, DOY, EL.KE., SDOE).
